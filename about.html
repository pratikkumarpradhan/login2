ABOUT PROJECTKART

Built for people who love to build.

We make it easier to find the electronics components, tools and project kits you need to bring your ideas to life.

OUR STORY

From an idea to a place for makers.

ProjectKart is built around a simple idea: electronics should be easier to explore, learn and build with.

Whether you are working on your first Arduino project, experimenting with sensors, developing a college project or creating your next DIY prototype, we aim to make finding the right components simple.

Our collection is focused on practical electronics components, development boards, sensors, modules, batteries, accessories and ready-to-build project kits.
