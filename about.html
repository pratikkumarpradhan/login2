ProjectKart is an online electronics and project-components business for students, makers, hobbyists and creators who learn, prototype and build.

A WORK IN PROGRESS, BY DESIGN

A considered starting point for every build.

ProjectKart serves students, engineers, makers, hobbyists, robotics enthusiasts, developers and DIY creators with the components behind their ideas.

ProjectKart is currently operated from home, serving customers online. We make it easy to discover the components you need and contact us directly whenever your project needs a little more guidance.

Business story: From Ideas to Real Projects ⚡ ProjectKart was created with one simple goal — to make project components easier to find, easier to understand, and easier to get. Whether you're a student building your first Arduino project, a hobbyist experimenting with electronics, or a maker bringing a new idea to life, ProjectKart brings the essential components you need together in one place. From Arduino boards and sensors to batteries, modules, wires, and other electronics components — we're here to help turn your ideas into something real. Think it. Build it. ProjectKart it.
